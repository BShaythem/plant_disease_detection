# Disease card — Peacock spot (olive leaf spot)

## Identification

- **Name (EN)**: Peacock spot (olive leaf spot)
- **Scientific name**: Venturia oleaginea (formerly Spilocaea oleagina; syn. Cycloconium oleaginum, Fusicladium oleagineum)
- **EPPO code**: CYCLOL
- **Pathogen type**: Fungus

## Symptoms

Circular dark green to black lesions on the upper leaf surface, usually surrounded by a yellow (chlorotic) halo. Premature leaf drop occurs under advanced infection.

*Source: Buonaurio et al. 2023, Frontiers in Plant Science*

## Favorable conditions

Wet, cool weather; spring or autumn rains; densely planted orchards with poor air circulation.

*Source: Pacific Northwest Pest Management Handbooks*

## Severity stages

| Stage | Description | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A few isolated spots on lower leaves | Monitoring |
| 2 | Numerous spots on several leaves, clear yellow halo | Treatment recommended |
| 3 | Leaf drop beginning | Urgent treatment |
| 4 | Significant defoliation, weakened branches | Urgent treatment + monitor tree vigor |

*Source: compiled by synthesis from Buonaurio et al. 2023 — not from a single official source. TO BE VALIDATED BY AN AGRONOMIST.*

## Treatment

### Copper-based fungicide (Bordeaux mixture or equivalent)
- **Dosage**: to confirm per commercial product and local regulations (as a rough indication, a half-dose ~5-6 g/L is often cited)
- **Frequency**: autumn treatment (before rains) and/or spring treatment
- **Precautions**: do not apply right before heavy rain (washes off); respect the pre-harvest interval
